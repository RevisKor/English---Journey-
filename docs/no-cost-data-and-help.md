# English Journey — Help, Privacy, and No-Cost Use

## How the learning tools work

English Journey stores your learning progress, quiz attempts, review queue, and optional writing/reading work so the course can show your next lesson and schedule revision. The browser’s built-in speech feature can read words and example sentences aloud when your device provides a voice. This does not send audio to a paid service.

The optional reading and writing prompts are copied by you into an AI tool that you choose. English Journey does not call a paid AI service on your behalf and does not receive the content you paste into an external tool.

## Preferences

You can choose a British or American English voice preference and whether the interface uses bilingual, English-first, or Arabic-first support. These preferences are saved in your learning profile.

## Your data

The platform stores only data needed for the learning experience: your account identity, course progress, XP and streak information, review items, assessment attempts, and study work you choose to submit. It does not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

## Keeping your data private

Avoid placing sensitive personal, financial, medical, or identifying details in practice writing or in prompts copied to external tools. Use fictional examples when practising personal topics.

## Getting help

Use the course guide in the learner header to understand the lesson sequence. For each lesson, begin with vocabulary, continue through guided grammar and practice, then complete reading, writing, and the contextual quiz. Use **Daily review** whenever you want to retrieve difficult language again.
