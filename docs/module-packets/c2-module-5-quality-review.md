# C2 Module 5 Quality Review

## Scope

C2 Module 5 (Lessons 61–75) is the interpretation-and-uncertainty arc. It moves from defending artistic readings to scientific explanation, belonging, crisis leadership, discourse framing, adaptation, narrative reliability, and accountable repair. The module extends the C2 emphasis on mediation and independent judgement without repeating the institutional-and-ethical sequence of Module 4.

## Pedagogical review

Each lesson has one primary objective and one explicit bilingual retrieval check. Retrieval asks for evidence or a transferable distinction rather than recognition of an isolated word. The activity corpus varies reading, interaction, listening, writing, grammar, speaking, review, and assessment routes; it does not impose a fixed skill rotation.

The module's difficulty grows through interpretive accountability, audience design, epistemic calibration, and ethical representation. The learner must distinguish observation from inference, preserve uncertainty, notice framing, and keep affected voices visible. The word bank remains supportive but the meaningful work is performed through source comparison, mediation, public explanation, and repair.

## Skill and archetype balance

The 15 lessons represent at least seven activity kinds. Reading occurs on Lessons 61, 66, 69, and 73, each with a distinct comprehension purpose. Listening occurs on Lessons 63 and 67 and keeps the transcript behind progressive disclosure. Writing occurs on Lessons 64 and 72, with an assessment writing/speaking option on Lesson 75. Grammar is isolated on Lessons 65 and 71 so the module does not pack every skill into every lesson. Interaction and speaking appear when negotiation, crisis response, or repair is the actual objective. Lesson 70 is a compact retrieval checkpoint, and Lesson 75 is the transfer assessment.

## Visual and disclosure review

Every experience supplies the four first-view answers: what this is, what to do, what matters, and what comes next. Density varies between light, normal, and deep. Supporting material such as Arabic help, worked examples, extended rationale, word support, and transcripts is revealed progressively. Listening routes explicitly preserve the first-pass challenge before transcript access. Semantic labels remain part of the shared visual language rather than relying on colour alone.

## Assessment review

The final assessment supplies a source pack containing an interpretation, an evidence summary, and an affected account. The learner produces either a public explainer or a spoken recommendation. The rubric implied by the prompt checks evidence fidelity, distinction between observation and inference, calibrated uncertainty, ethical representation, audience appropriateness, register, cohesion, and a visible revision trigger. External-AI prompts remain optional and no per-request grading API is required.

## Engineering evidence
