# C1 Module 1 Quality Review

## Scope

C1 Module 1 (Lessons 1–16) is reauthored as **Ideas & Evidence Studio**, with one primary objective per lesson and a short bilingual retrieval check in every authored activity.

## Pedagogical review

The sixteen-lesson arc moves from implication and claim scrutiny through calibrated uncertainty, source disagreement, public testimony, ethical counterfactuals, audience adaptation, expert attribution, and a source-aware public brief. The sequence is intentionally non-rotational: reading appears where source comparison is the objective, writing follows evidence-building rather than a fixed slot, listening is used for uncertainty and audience adaptation, and interaction/speaking make abstract judgement socially accountable.

Reading occurs frequently across the module, writing appears at Lessons 5, 9, and 15, listening appears at Lessons 3 and 13, and speaking/interaction are distributed at Lessons 6, 7, and 11. Lesson 12 is a lower-load review route, while Lesson 16 is a cumulative assessment. The module therefore increases synthesis without packing every skill into each lesson.

## Bilingual and retrieval review

Titles, objectives, activity prompts, and retrieval prompts are supplied in English and Arabic. Retrieval asks learners to reuse an evidence, attribution, qualification, or counterargument move from B2 in a new C1 context. The check is short enough to preserve lesson compactness and specific enough to expose transfer.

## Visual and disclosure review

Each lesson has a first-view record answering what the lesson is, what the learner does, what matters, and what comes next. Audio-first lessons use a speaking-line payload and keep transcript-style support subordinate to listening. Progressive supports distinguish word support, worked examples, tips, extended rationale, external-AI prompt guidance, and transcript disclosure rather than presenting all detail at once.

## Assessment review

Lesson 16 requires a source-aware public brief that explains a finding, qualifies uncertainty, compares trade-offs, attributes evidence, recommends action, and names a review condition. This is an appropriate C1 transfer task because it evaluates responsible synthesis rather than isolated form recall. External AI remains an optional grading route through the prompt-first workflow; no per-request AI service was added.

## Engineering evidence

Focused C1 curriculum tests cover the sixteen authored lessons, activity presence, adaptive archetype diversity, bilingual retrieval, and the listening route. Full TypeScript, production build, curriculum audit, rearchitecture audit, and controlled Vitest batches remain required before checkpointing.

## Decision

**Ready for full validation**, subject to the existing unrelated external database SSL startup warning remaining isolated from curriculum compilation and tests.
