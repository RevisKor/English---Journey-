# B2 Module 8 Quality Review

## Scope

This review covers Lessons 106–120 after the six-agent architecture, authoring, integration, and focused regression stages. The module remains compact and objective-led: each lesson has one dominant learner job and one short bilingual retrieval check. The corpus does not force vocabulary, grammar, reading, writing, speaking, listening, interaction, and assessment into every lesson.

## Pedagogical review

The lesson arc moves from calibrated risk language and source trust into compromise, consequence, access, translation, belonging, public-policy evidence, automated decisions, review writing, respectful disagreement, prediction, workplace wellbeing, heritage compromise, and final position defence. This progression gives B2 learners repeated exposure to qualification, concession, evidence, counterargument, and conditional recommendation without repeating one lesson template.

Reading is distributed through Lessons 107, 110, 114, and 118. Writing appears in Lessons 111, 113, and 115. Interaction appears in Lessons 108, 116, and 119. Grammar-led work appears in Lessons 106, 109, and 117. Lesson 112 is a distinct listening route focused on identifying a claim, qualification, and exclusion question. Review and assessment are present without making every lesson an assessment.

The retrieval wrapper is intentionally brief. It asks the learner to retrieve one decision or discourse move from the current lesson and provide concise evidence serving the primary objective. This prevents retrieval from becoming a second lesson while preserving the owner-defined requirement that every lesson has a check.

## Visual and progressive-disclosure review

The authored data uses semantic labels and bilingual objective fields so the learner can understand what the lesson is, what to do, and what matters before opening optional detail. Reading lessons receive word support and Arabic help. Assessment lessons receive worked examples and tips. Other lessons receive only the compact supports needed for their route. The listening lesson uses the existing audio-first workspace contract with transcript disclosure rather than placing a full transcript in the first view.

The interface remains calm and does not use colour alone to communicate activity type. The lesson route is varied in content while retaining consistent navigation and accessible labels. No new visual implementation was required for the Module 8 content change; the existing workspace renderer and regression patterns remain the visual control.

## Assessment review

Lesson 113 asks the learner to build a strong evidence-based public-policy case with evidence points, concession, rebuttal, recommendation, and review measure. Lesson 120 serves as the module checkpoint by asking for a concise persuasive response to a public-choice issue with two views, a valid concession, cohesive links, and a qualified recommendation. These assessments measure transfer rather than simple recognition and remain proportionate to B2 writing expectations.

The assessment routes are separated from the shorter grammar, reading, listening, interaction, and review routes. Retrieval checks remain supportive and formative; they do not replace the larger assessment tasks.

## Gate decision

**Pass with the compact-lesson controls retained.** Module 8 is ready for the full engineering gate after workspace coverage confirms the listening route and staged fallback begins at Lesson 121. The existing development database SSL startup-sync warning is operationally unrelated to this curriculum packet and is not treated as a content failure.
