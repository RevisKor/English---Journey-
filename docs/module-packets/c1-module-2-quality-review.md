# C1 Module 2 Quality Review — Institutional Voice and Accountable Response

## Review scope

C1 Module 2 (Lessons 17–32) reworks the authoritative draft into sixteen compact bilingual learner journeys. The module centres on institutional voice, responsibility tracing, calibrated attribution, public explanation, audience adaptation, and accountable recommendation. Each lesson retains one primary objective and an explicit Arabic/English retrieval check.

## Pedagogical review

The route does not use a fixed activity rotation. It opens with a light distinction between institutional position and personal recommendation, moves into responsibility-tracing and source comparison, then spaces grammar, listening, interaction, vocabulary, writing, review, speaking, mediation, and assessment according to the objective. Reading appears in Lessons 18, 23, and 30; writing appears in Lessons 24 and 31; listening appears in Lessons 20 and 27; interaction appears in Lesson 21; and a dedicated assessment closes Lesson 32. This satisfies the C1 need for frequent source work and synthesis while avoiding an artificial reading–writing–speaking–listening sequence.

The module increases depth through transfer rather than content packing. Learners first recognise institutional stance, then trace responsibility, calibrate claims, clarify disagreement, mediate technical language, revise for audience, and finally synthesize paired sources into a public response. Lessons 24, 31, and 32 are the heavier production points; the vocabulary and review lessons deliberately lower density so retrieval can consolidate the discourse moves.

Every authored lesson has one retrieval record with bilingual prompt and expected evidence. Retrieval asks the learner to reuse attribution, qualification, concession, or audience-shift moves in a new institutional context rather than merely repeat a definition.

## Visual and disclosure review

Every experience includes first-view guidance answering what the lesson is, what to do, what matters, and what comes next. Listening routes in Lessons 20 and 27 are audio-first in the activity contract, with transcript support listed as progressive disclosure rather than as the first task. Reading routes use word support and Arabic help without placing all explanation in the initial view. Writing routes expose worked examples, external-AI grading prompts, and extended rationale as supports rather than mandatory content. The semantic archetype label remains distinct from colour and is carried by the shared workspace.

## Assessment review

Lesson 32 is a cumulative, source-aware assessment. It requires identification of disagreement, attribution of multiple claims, a limitation, a proportionate recommendation, and an explicit review trigger. The assessment therefore measures transfer across the module rather than isolated grammar recall. The experience map marks this route as deep and includes the next-bridge stage so the learner records a transferable strength before Module 3.
