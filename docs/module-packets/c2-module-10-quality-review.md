# C2 Module 10 Quality Review

## Scope

Module 10 covers Lessons 136–150 and serves as the C2 capstone. Its arc moves from whole-argument synthesis through cross-genre mediation, audience design, qualified authority, objection handling, public explanation, revision, rehearsal, and the final level challenge.

## Pedagogical review

Each lesson has one primary objective and one explicit bilingual retrieval check. The module deliberately varies the dominant experience rather than rotating skills mechanically: review and mapping begin the arc; reading, grammar, listening, interaction, speaking, and writing are used where the capstone demands them; review and assessment close the course. Retrieval asks learners to reactivate prior C2 and C1 habits without forcing unrelated vocabulary into the task.

The final three lessons progressively increase consequence. Lesson 148 rehearses the success criteria, Lesson 149 requires a position under scrutiny, and Lesson 150 asks for independent synthesis across a mixed source pack. The final assessment therefore measures transfer, qualification, audience awareness, and responsible mediation rather than surface complexity.

## Bilingual and disclosure review

Learner-facing objective, guidance, retrieval, and support fields remain available in English and Arabic. Listening lessons include progressive transcript support rather than exposing the transcript on first view. External-AI writing prompts remain explicit and cost-neutral: the site stores the prompt and learner work, while grading can be performed in the learner's chosen external tool.

The first-view experience consistently answers four questions: what the activity is, what to do, what matters, and what comes next. Detailed rationales, worked examples, Arabic help, and transcript support are progressively disclosed so the C2 learner receives depth without an undifferentiated wall of text.

## Assessment review

Lesson 149 is an accountable position-under-scrutiny assessment. Lesson 150 is the terminal C2 level challenge. Both require the learner to integrate evidence, uncertainty, competing perspectives, objections, audience design, and a review condition. The final lesson intentionally omits a next-bridge stage because it is the terminal authored lesson for the current C2 course.

## Engineering review

The Module 10 corpus and experience map use the established shared contracts and highest-precedence pipeline integration for Lessons 136–150. Focused C2 tests cover 180-lesson integrity, 13 C2 regression cases, authored activity diversity, bilingual retrieval, reading and writing cadence, listening transcript disclosure, capstone assessment semantics, and the six-step C1 retrieval route. The remaining release gate is the complete Vitest suite, TypeScript check, production build, curriculum audit, and rearchitecture audit.
