# C1 Module 3 Quality Review

## Scope

C1 Module 3 covers Lessons 33–48 and extends the C1 Ideas & Evidence Studio from source interpretation into public-policy synthesis. The module is authored as sixteen compact bilingual learner journeys. Each lesson keeps one primary objective and includes an explicit English/Arabic retrieval check. The module closes with a cumulative assessment that asks the learner to interpret competing narratives and propose respectful institutional change.

## Pedagogical review

The architecture uses a non-rotational sequence: source comparison, omission mapping, audio calibration, evidential grammar, mediation, paired-source reading, causal language, bounded explanation, counterfactual speaking, ethical-condition listening, policy/community synthesis, formal recommendation, retrieval, integrated proposal, and two assessment routes. Reading appears frequently, writing is spaced across lessons 40, 44, 46, and 48, listening is audio-first at 35 and 42, speaking and interaction are distributed where the reasoning task requires them, and review is placed before the capstone rather than forced into a mechanical rotation.

The lesson records preserve the C1 level’s emphasis on qualified claims, attribution, causal relationships, conditional reasoning, audience awareness, consent, and institutional responsibility. Arabic support is present in titles, objectives, source passages, speaking lines, writing prompts, and retrieval checks. No lesson is designed as an all-skills bundle; supporting stages are selected only when they serve the primary objective.

## Visual and disclosure review

The experience map gives every lesson a visible answer to four learner questions: what this is, what to do, what matters, and what comes next. Semantic archetype labels remain paired with text and icon treatment in the existing workspace. Listening lessons keep the spoken route primary and place transcript support behind progressive disclosure. Reading and writing support is similarly staged through word support, Arabic help, worked examples, and external-AI prompt guidance rather than exposing every explanation at once.

The module does not introduce a new visual system. It uses the established calm neutral palette, semantic activity language, bilingual directionality, and first-view guidance contract. The richer C1 content is therefore carried by progressive disclosure and source complexity rather than by denser visual decoration.

## Assessment review

Retrieval is explicit in all sixteen authored activities. Lesson 45 provides a dedicated evidence-move review, Lessons 47 and 48 provide cumulative assessment routes, and the final route requires source attribution, uncertainty management, counterargument handling, consent, and a proportionate proposal. External-AI prompts are used only as learner-controlled writing feedback instructions; no per-request model cost or hidden grading dependency is introduced.

## Engineering acceptance criteria

The module is acceptable when the authored corpus and experience map typecheck, C1 tests verify sixteen populated lessons with diverse archetypes and bilingual retrieval, the learner workspace renders the authored listening and assessment routes, the first un-authored boundary advances to Lesson 49, the curriculum and rearchitecture audits remain clean, and the production build succeeds. The existing external database SSL startup warning is unrelated to this curriculum change and remains documented separately.
