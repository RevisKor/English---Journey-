# C2 Module 4 Quality Review

## Scope

C2 Module 4 (Lessons 46–60) is the institutional-and-ethical judgement arc. It moves from institutional language and competing obligations toward ethical framing, public accountability, mediated evidence, and a final transfer assessment. The module preserves the C2 promise: advanced learners are not merely shown difficult vocabulary; they are asked to make consequential language choices for defined audiences.

## Pedagogical review

Each lesson has one primary objective and one explicit retrieval check. Retrieval prompts are bilingual and require evidence, so the learner must recover a prior C1 or C2 idea rather than recognize a familiar word passively. The activity corpus varies the dominant route across mediation, source comparison, reading, listening, interaction, writing, review, and assessment. It does not force a fixed reading–writing–speaking–listening rotation.

The module’s difficulty is increased through abstraction, audience responsibility, qualification, and institutional consequence. Vocabulary is presented with Arabic support, examples, collocations, related terms, and register information, while the lesson-level activity asks the learner to use those resources in context. Writing routes use external-AI grading prompts rather than an in-product per-request AI dependency. Listening routes disclose transcripts progressively rather than exposing them by default.

## Skill and archetype balance

The 15 lessons contain a deliberately varied set of activity kinds with at least seven distinct routes represented. Reading and writing appear repeatedly but are not universal. Listening is present on authentic lessons and includes transcript disclosure. Interaction and speaking are used where the objective requires audience negotiation or institutional response. The final lesson is an assessment/transfer route rather than another content-heavy lesson.

## Visual and disclosure review

The experience map gives every lesson a bilingual first view answering four learner questions: what this is, what to do, what matters, and what comes next. Density and stage sequencing vary by objective. Supporting material such as transcripts, model language, and deeper source notes is progressively disclosed so the interface remains calm and the C2 learner can choose when to inspect evidence.

Semantic labels and activity identity remain explicit in addition to colour. The experience map uses the established shared contract and does not introduce a second navigation model. The learner workspace continues to retain a tested legacy fallback for lessons without an authored experience.

## Assessment review

The final lesson is a transfer assessment that asks the learner to combine precision, evidence handling, ethical qualification, and audience-aware communication. Its retrieval record, assessment semantic, and adaptive assessment archetype are present. Earlier lessons provide smaller checks so the module assessment is cumulative without packing every skill into every lesson.

## Engineering evidence

The Module 4 corpus, experience map, C2 pipeline integration, and focused regressions compile and pass. The complete release gate passed with TypeScript validation, production build, curriculum audit, rearchitecture audit, and the full Vitest suite: **50 test files and 274 tests passed**. The recurring development database SSL startup synchronization warning remains unrelated to curriculum source changes and does not affect the deterministic release gate.

## Decision

C2 Module 4 is approved for checkpointing. The next queued curriculum milestone is C2 Module 5; Vercel credential-dependent verification items remain separate and are not marked complete by this source-level validation.
