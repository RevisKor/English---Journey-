# C2 Module 12 Architecture

Module 12 (Lessons 166–180) is the terminal extension of C2: knowledge under pressure, expert limits, trustworthy recommendations, competing futures, public explanation, accountable decision logs, oral defence, public demonstration, readiness review, and a lifelong language plan.

The module treats advanced English as responsible action. It varies reading, listening, interaction, speaking, writing, review, and assessment according to the objective rather than forcing a fixed sequence. Lesson 179 audits C2 control; Lesson 180 turns that control into a sustainable next-stage practice plan.
