## Gate 4 — Pedagogical QA

| Review question | Evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Does every lesson have an objective-led dominant experience rather than a rotation slot? | Lessons 46–60 each carry a selected archetype, density, first-view framing, and activity tied to the module packet. | Does the module develop a coherent B2 performance arc? | Lessons 46–48 establish precision, consequence, and a defensible public position; Lessons 49–53 examine source limits, claim calibration, attribution, and data; Lessons 54–58 turn those tools into spoken and written public judgement; Lesson 59 retrieves the evidence trail; Lesson 60 transfers it to a conditional compromise. | **PASS** |

| Is Arabic support purposeful for Arabic-speaking learners? | Activity objectives, prompts, key spoken frames, and evidence questions are bilingual. Analytical reading and learner performance stay English-forward, preserving B2 challenge without removing orientation support. | **PASS** |
| Are neighbouring routes mechanically varied? | The corpus moves through grammar repair, consultation interaction, formal writing, verification reading, vocabulary calibration, a staff panel, listening for responsibility with transcript disclosure, data analysis, speaking, access design, invitation revision, synthesis, risk communication, review, and assessment. ## Review outcome and required engineering gate

The three specialist reviews pass because the module has a traceable architecture, compact objective-led routes, explicit one-check retrieval evidence, a bounded listening-first experience, progressive first-view framing, and a proportionate evidence-led transfer assessment. The owner-defined skill ranges are treated as balancing controls: B2 reading is frequent, writing is spaced, listening is increasingly authentic, speaking and real-world tasks recur without forming a fixed rotation, and Lesson 60 remains the module assessment. It becomes complete only when Engineering Gate 7 records focused regressions, controlled application and shared-course batches, TypeScript, production build, curriculum audit, rearchitecture audit, and visual verification.
