# B2 Module 7 Quality Review

## Scope

B2 Module 7 covers Lessons 91–105. Its authored arc moves through nuanced appraisal, respectful disagreement, formal recommendation, pressured briefings, preservation and change, qualified public positions, certainty calibration, convenience and cost, workplace policy, system repair, statistical boundaries, counterfactual decisions, public-space proposals, missing voices, and a final case for change.

## Pedagogical review

Every authored lesson has one primary objective and one short bilingual retrieval/check record. The route sequence is deliberately varied rather than rotational: reading, interaction, grammar, listening, reading, speaking, listening, writing, interaction, review, reading, interaction, writing, speaking, assessment. Reading occurs regularly for B2 source interpretation; writing occurs at Lessons 98 and 103 before the final assessment; listening is distinct at Lessons 94 and 97; speaking is reserved for public-position and missing-evidence production; and grammar is concentrated in Lesson 93 rather than repeated as the dominant focus.

Ordinary lessons remain compact. Reading lessons use a bounded source and one interpretive purpose. Listening lessons use audio-first attention with transcript support available progressively. Writing lessons specify audience, length, and rhetorical constraints. Speaking lessons provide a short production frame. The integrated Lesson 105 assessment is the only route that deliberately combines evidence, concession, refutation, and recommendation.

Retrieval is continuous but low-stakes. Each lesson’s check is tied to its own objective, Lesson 100 provides a compact review, and Lesson 105 provides transfer assessment. The module therefore supports retention without turning every lesson into a large quiz.

## Visual and disclosure review

The existing calm learner workspace is reused with bilingual semantic labels. The first view answers what the activity is, what to do, what matters, and what comes next. Reading, writing, interaction, grammar, speaking, review, and assessment expose only the information needed for the current route. Listening Lessons 94 and 97 use the audio-first presentation; transcript and worked-example support remain available without replacing the listening challenge. Activity identity is communicated through label, icon, and semantic styling rather than colour alone.

## Assessment review

Lesson 100 retrieves reporting, evaluation, and consequence language before the later source work. Lesson 105 is the module assessment: the learner writes a qualified case for or against a reform using two evidence points, one concession, one refutation, a qualified recommendation, and a review measure. This assesses responsible transfer rather than recognition alone and is appropriately separated from ordinary lesson scope.

## Validation evidence

Focused B2 and learner-workspace regressions passed with 46 checks. Module 7 authored precedence, explicit retrieval checks, reading and listening routes, the assessment payload, and staged fallback to Lesson 121 are protected. TypeScript, production build, curriculum audit, and rearchitecture audit are required before checkpointing. The existing development database SSL startup-sync warning is unrelated to this curriculum work.

## Gate decision

**PASS pending the full engineering gate.** Module 7 meets the compact-lesson rule, explicit retrieval contract, non-rotational cadence, staged skill layering, and listening-disclosure requirements.
