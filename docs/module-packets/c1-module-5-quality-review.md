# C1 Module 5 Quality Review

## Scope

This review covers Lessons 65–80, an integrity-and-formal-proposal arc grounded in the authoritative C1 draft and the Module 5 architecture packet.

## Pedagogical review

Each lesson has one primary objective. The sequence moves from identifying competing values and distinguishing risk from wrongdoing through safeguards, formal objections, negotiation, audit trails, dissent, recommendations, proportionality, and a final integrity proposal checkpoint. Retrieval is explicit and bilingual in every authored lesson, with calibrated uncertainty and responsible reporting language revisited without forcing identical activity layouts.

The skill cadence is intentionally non-rotational: reading and source interpretation recur throughout; listening is audio-first in Lessons 67 and 74; interaction and speaking are placed where learners must negotiate, respond, facilitate, and brief; writing escalates from a formal objection to a complete proposal and final recommendation. The module closes with an evidence-aware written and spoken assessment.

## Visual and disclosure review

Experiences use semantic archetype labels, first-view guidance, density variation, and progressive disclosure. Listening routes reveal the transcript only after an audio-first attempt. Detailed support remains available without overwhelming the first view. The workspace continues to use the shared visual language with text labels and accessible cues rather than colour alone.

## Assessment review

Assessment evidence samples interpretation, calibrated claims, dialogue, formal writing, and oral synthesis. Retrieval checks are short, bilingual, and tied to the current objective. The final checkpoint requires the learner to connect evidence, uncertainty, safeguards, implementation, and review rather than reproduce isolated terminology.

## Engineering evidence

Focused C1 and learner-workspace regressions pass after the staged fallback was advanced to Lesson 81. The full TypeScript, production build, curriculum audit, rearchitecture audit, and controlled Vitest gate remains the release criterion for checkpointing. The known external database SSL startup warning is unrelated to authored curriculum content.

## Decision

Module 5 is pedagogically coherent and ready for the full engineering gate, subject to the explicit validation commands and checkpoint review required by the operating system.
