## Design implications

1. Build each level around **observable performance** rather than a checklist of grammar rules.
2. Introduce vocabulary as **word families, collocations, phrases, and discourse patterns** after A1; do not constrain the sequence by first letter.
3. Require planned retrieval of earlier material in later reading, writing, speaking, and assessment tasks.
4. Gradually reduce Arabic scaffolding: rich bilingual support at A2, targeted Arabic contrast at B1/B2, and English-first analysis with optional Arabic rescue support at C1/C2.
5. Treat the published hour figures as **planning ranges rather than promises**; the platform should present weekly pathways and milestone evidence, not fixed completion guarantees.
6. Use Oxford/Cambridge resources as **curriculum-validation references**, while authoring original definitions, examples, passages, and tasks to respect publisher copyright and terms of use.
